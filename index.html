Train an agent. Play the cards.

PokeAgent is a deep-learning project for the Kaggle PTCG AI Battle Challenge. We train and compare three agents — behaviour cloning, PPO, and a value-based search agent — that play the Pokémon Trading Card Game turn by turn.

01 · MODELS

Three agents, one game.

We train three different agents and compare them head to head, with one serving as the benchmark for the others.

AGENT 01

Behaviour cloning

Learns from recorded games: given the board state, it predicts the action a strong player would take.

AGENT 02

PPO

Learns by playing: a policy trained with Proximal Policy Optimization through self-play and matches against baseline bots.

AGENT 03

Value-based search

Learns to score board positions, then searches ahead over possible moves and picks the line with the best expected outcome.
